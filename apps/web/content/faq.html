GovDex FAQ

What is GovDex?

GovDex is a directory of the public bodies that govern each address, and the people who sit on them. Enter an address and it returns your county, your city or town, your school district, the legislative and judicial districts you sit in, and the smaller boards beneath them. Each entry lists who holds the seat and how to reach them.

Where did the name come from?

It is short for Governance Index. “Governance” is deliberately broader than “government.” It leaves room to cover party committees and other civic bodies that hold real power but are not agencies.

Is GovDex a way to email my representatives?

No. It is a directory. We do not want it used to generate more email. Email does not scale, and officials in practice hear from a small, self-selected slice of the people they serve. GovDex is a first step toward better ways for residents and public bodies to deliberate in the open.

Where does the data come from?

Public data seeds the state, county, and municipal layers. Volunteers fill in the local detail no dataset reaches: small boards, party committees, channels, and software. GovDex still depends on public bodies posting their information. Collection only goes so far.

What area does it cover?

New York first. We'll be loading other states in the weeks to come.

How can I help?

Volunteers verify local bodies, the people in them, their channels, and their software. If you know your town, you can help map it.
