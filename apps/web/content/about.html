About GovDex

GovDex aims to be a universal directory for governance related information.

We are aiming to collect the data from the state level to the county and then to municipal levels, including advisory boards. Our breadth of coverage is looking at not only who represents you, but provide quick access to key resources (budget, calendars, meeting agendas, and project pages if available), but available communications channels, and even the software tools used by these bodies.
