Key Resources

Indicates if these key resources are available online.

  • Laws & Policies: Code, laws, bylaws, regulations, or policies.
  • Budget: Current, past, and proposed budgets.
  • Agendas & Minutes: What a body will take up at its meetings and what it decided.
  • Calendar: Upcoming meetings and events.
  • Announcements: Notices of specific changes, such as road closures, schedule changes, or new rules.
  • Directory: Who holds each seat and how to reach them.
  • Projects: Status of current work: planned, active, complete, or cancelled.

Tool types

The kinds of software an entity uses to operate.

  • Presence: Managing the website.
  • Participation: Commenting, surveys, and forums. See the Participatory Governance Framework.
  • Proceedings: Tools for running meetings: agenda management, minutes, video streaming, and tracking issues.
  • Operations: Back-office tools for finance, membership, staffing, and scheduling.
  • Services: Tools for delivering services to residents, such as permits, service requests, licenses, and benefits. Government only.
  • Transparency: Public disclosure systems, such as open-data portals and public records request systems. Government only.
  • Oversight: Tools for audit, ethics, and compliance. Government only.
